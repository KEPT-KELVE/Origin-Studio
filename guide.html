THE ORIGIN STUDIO GUIDE / VERSION 1.1

A place to start.

Get your first photo into the workspace, build with layers and masks, then export it with the details you choose.

InstallFirst editLayers & masksColourMetadata & GPSExportShortcuts

01 / Install or run.

Use OriginStudio-Setup-1.1.exe to install the app and add desktop and Start menu shortcuts. Installation is for your Windows user.

For the standalone edition, run OriginStudio-1.1.exe directly. No extraction or setup is needed. Both editions require Windows 10 or 11 and .NET Framework 4.8.

Upgrading? Save your project, close the older version, run the new installer and open the updated shortcut.

02 / Your first edit.

  1. Click Open image or drag a photo into the app. Its existing metadata appears in the inspector.
  2. Click Editing, then Lift photo. The original becomes an editable image layer.
  3. Select a layer in the bottom stack. Use Layer for masks and transforms, or Color for colour changes.
  4. Click + Image to add another photo as a separate layer.
  5. Choose Save project to keep your work editable, or Export to save a JPEG or PNG.

The upper rows of the layer stack appear above the lower ones. Select the layer you want to edit before changing its settings.

03 / Layers, masks and cutouts.

In the Layer tab, rename, duplicate, reorder, hide or lock a layer. Change its opacity or blend mode. Expand Transform & align to set exact position, size and rotation.

Put a photo inside a shape

  1. Import a transparent PNG silhouette, or draw a filled shape.
  2. Import the photo that should appear inside it.
  3. Put the photo directly above the silhouette in the stack.
  4. Select the photo and enable Clip below.
  5. Move or resize the photo until the part you want sits inside the shape.

The silhouette must stay visible. A fully opaque JPEG produces a rectangular clipping boundary.

Use an image as a mask

Select your image layer and open Mask & cutout. Choose Alpha to use a PNG’s transparency, Luminance to reveal light areas, or Dark silhouette to reveal dark areas. Click Use image as mask and choose the mask file.

Use Paint hide and Paint reveal to refine the result. Brush size and opacity are above the canvas; mask softness is in the mask section. You can also invert, feather, disable or remove the mask.

Remove a background colour

Choose the colour with the eyedropper or colour picker, then expand Remove a background colour. Set tolerance and edge fade, and apply the cutout to the selected layer. This produces a mask, so the original pixels remain available.

Masks are limited to 2,048 pixels on their longest edge. The underlying photo retains its original resolution. Image-layer masks transform with the image.

04 / Find your colours.

The Color tab applies settings to the selected layer. With the background selected, the controls adjust the original background.

Use twenty controls across light and tone, white balance, hue, colour channels, sepia, blur, sharpen and mosaic. Start from a built-in look or save your settings as a .grade file.

Change only part of a photo

  1. Click + Adjustment to add an adjustment layer.
  2. Set its colour controls in Color.
  3. Switch to Layer and choose Hide all.
  4. Use Paint reveal over the areas that should receive the adjustment.

An adjustment layer changes visible content beneath it. Layers above it stay unchanged. Adjustment-layer masks cover the document.

05 / Edit the details.

Opening a photo fills Metadata with its actual embedded values. Missing fields are left blank. Browsing the preset list changes nothing until you click Apply selected device.

Choose a phone to fill friendly device names, a lens maker and camera label, plus generated sample software and body-serial values. Use Generate beside Software or Serial for a new value. Every field is editable; custom brands and models work too.

Generated software, lens labels and serials are synthetic editing samples. They are not verified firmware, calibrated optical specifications or real device identifiers. The preset catalog is bundled and does not update automatically.

Dates and exposure

Enter the full date and time together, using a 24-hour clock. Leave a field blank to remove that tag on export.

Choose a location

Open Location Studio to click a map point, search for a place or enter coordinates. Its Google Maps button opens your browser; copy the chosen point’s coordinates or full map link back into Location Studio.

Photo processing is local. Map tiles and place searches use the internet and send location queries to the relevant services.

06 / Save the result.

Export writes a separate JPEG or PNG with supported EXIF and XMP. PNG preserves transparency. JPEG fills transparent areas with white. Metadata-only JPEG exports can preserve the original compressed pixels when there are no visual edits or resizing.

Save project stores source pixels, image layers, masks, colour settings and metadata in an .origin file. Version 1.1 opens 1.0 projects; projects saved by 1.1 need 1.1 or later.

Crop and the Image menu’s canvas rotation/flip flatten the composition. Undo restores the previous layers. For independent layer transforms, use the Layer tab. Undo history is not stored in saved projects.

Import JPEG, PNG, BMP, GIF (one still frame) and TIFF. HEIC, RAW, PSD, video and professional ICC/CMYK workflows are not included in 1.1. Export rebuilds supported EXIF and common XMP; proprietary MakerNotes, old embedded previews and unmapped XMP are not retained.
